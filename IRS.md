Rubicon UI Team Accomplishment: Interest Rate Swaps Platform Modernization
Overview
The Rubicon Rates UI team has successfully migrated the Interest Rate Swaps (IRS) trading platform from a legacy .NET WPF desktop application to a modern, OpenFin-based web solution. This migration delivers a series of high-impact enhancements that strengthen front-office workflow efficiency and position the Rates desk for continued innovation.
Recent Enhancements
The team most recently launched support for MAC Rolls and Curves within the OpenFin UI and integrated Bloomberg Compression via REM (Request for Market). Together, these capabilities enable advanced IRS trading scenarios—including Market Agreed Coupon (MAC) swaps and portfolio compression—directly through Bloomberg connectivity. The result is real-time, low-latency execution aligned with front-office needs, reducing manual touchpoints and improving trade processing speed across the desk. Alongside these features, targeted bug fixes have improved overall platform stability and user confidence.
Why OpenFin & Modern MFE Architecture Over Legacy .NET WPF
The migration replaces a monolithic, difficult-to-maintain .NET WPF desktop application with a modern, web-based Micro Frontend (MFE) architecture built on Angular and NX. This shift delivers tangible advantages:

Faster release cycles through streamlined CI/CD and independent deployment of micro frontends, rather than monolithic desktop releases.
Simplified deployment with no desktop installation or per-machine update overhead—new capabilities reach traders instantly via the web.
Consistent cross-platform experience across trader workstations through the OpenFin runtime.
Rapid iteration on trader feedback, enabled by a modular codebase and seamless integration with enterprise APIs and services.
Reuse of shared component libraries across the broader Rates platform, reducing duplicated development effort and accelerating time-to-market for future enhancements.

Strategic Context
These efforts are part of a broader initiative to modernize the Capital Markets Rates technology platform, with ongoing work to expand electronic trading coverage and enable data-driven automation for pricing and execution workflows.
